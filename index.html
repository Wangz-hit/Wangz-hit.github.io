PHD RESEARCHER · INDUSTRIAL AI

I am a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at Harbin Institute of Technology. My research explores knowledge- and physics-enhanced machine learning, industrial large language models, anomaly detection, and prognostics, with an emphasis on reliable reasoning and decision support for intelligent operations and maintenance.

I am advised by Xuyun Fu and Minghang Zhao at Harbin Institute of Technology.
